# Bundled assets and their licences

## Original artwork (project licence, GPL-3.0-or-later)

- **Logo**: a controller with four coloured buttons spelling P A W Z as the toes of a paw, drawn for pawz: `ui/assets/logo.svg` for the interface and `logo.ico` (16 to 256 px) for the executable. Both are made from `design/logo/logo.svg` by `scripts/update-logo.py`, framed square and without PowerPoint's invisible text layer. Working files (PSD, SVG, drafts) live in the git-ignored `design/` folder.
- **Controller drawings** for the models the Gamepad Asset Pack does not cover (Xbox Adaptive Controller, Logitech F310/F510/F710): stylized SVGs under `controllers/models/`, with no manufacturer image or logo. The other models use the asset pack's silhouettes (below). The drawings are not a hardware certification or calibration plans. `scripts/generate-controller-illustrations.py` regenerates them and the four X/Y axis icons under `controllers/input-icons/`.
- **Action icons** (pencil, cross, plus, duplicate, copy, check, settings, profiles, website) under `ui/assets/icons/`, with vector disabled variants.

Brand names identify controller models; they imply no affiliation.

## AL2009man libraries (MIT)

- **148 button SVGs** from [Gamepad Prompt Asset Pack](https://github.com/AL2009man/Gamepad-Prompt-Asset-Pack), revision `552d6448ffc4a6f84d488454878357ea375a59bd`, under `ui/assets/controllers/prompts/`: Shared, Nintendo, PlayStation and Xbox buttons, sticks, triggers, D-pads, paddles and context keys. Platform-specific Guide buttons are excluded, following the author's redistribution exception.
- **Detailed controller drawings** from the same [Gamepad Asset Pack](https://github.com/AL2009man/Gamepad-Asset-Pack) (`Controller Asset Pack/…/Theme SVG/`), shipped as `controllers/library/*-detailed.svg` and used by default because they show the shoulders and triggers: Switch Pro, Joy-Con, DualShock 4 (V2), DualSense, Xbox 360, Xbox One, Xbox One S, Xbox Series (Elite models use the Xbox One and Series ones), Steam Controller and Steam Deck. Logos and platform marks are removed when they are vendored, as trademarks are not redistributed: the Nintendo Switch logo, the PS icon (the DualSense's PS button is the logo itself, so that drawing has no Guide button), Microsoft and Xbox 360 texts, the Xbox logo on Guide buttons, the Steam logo and the STEAM wordmark. The Steam Deck's chroma-key green screen is removed and its triggers, hidden in the original, are shown. `scripts/vendor-controller-assets.py` lists each change; the manifest records them with the upstream and the stored SHA-256. The drawings still reproduce the shape of each manufacturer's controller, like the silhouettes reproduce their outlines.
- **Controller silhouettes** from [Gamepad Asset Pack](https://github.com/AL2009man/Gamepad-Asset-Pack), revision `a6d1113b0b3b2438bbbcb08a33f9a25eb2391d7c`, folder `Connection Icon Pack/Controller Type/SVG/`: the generic controller (`library-generic.svg`) and, under `controllers/library/`, Xbox 360, Xbox Wireless (Xbox One, One S, Series, Elite), DualShock 3, DualShock 4, DualSense (and Edge), Switch Pro, Joy-Con, Joy-Con grip, Steam Controller and Steam Deck. They are also the fallback for custom models of the Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo families, and stay available in Change illustration for the models that use a detailed drawing. The files are stored unmodified; the controller styles recolour and outline them in memory. `controllers/named/library/` holds copies of eight of them (DualSense, DualShock 3 and 4, Joy-Con, Steam Controller, Switch Pro, Xbox 360, Xbox Wireless) and of every detailed drawing whose buttons, sticks and d-pad carry their logical input as `id` and Inkscape layer name, made with Name inputs and Auto-detect; only those attributes differ from the originals, which pawz then uses in their place for the live display. They are derived works under the same MIT licence. The author asks for credit and advises against using the pack in commercially released products, since many images derive from official sources: pawz is a free GPL project, and anyone selling a build should replace them.

Author: **Al. Lopez / AL2009man**. Full MIT licences: `controllers/prompts/LICENSE.txt` and `controllers/Gamepad-Asset-Pack-LICENSE.txt`; the sub-pack README is kept. Files are stored unmodified; Qt selects the "Style - Outline (Black)" layer and composes combinations in memory. Prompt glyphs are vector paths, so Open Sans (which the author cites as the source of the letters) need not be installed. Coloured `-Color.svg` variants are preferred when they exist; colours never change logical ids or actions.

`controllers/upstream-manifest.json` records the exact revision URL and SHA-256 of each file; `scripts/vendor-controller-assets.py` reproduces the download. Nothing is downloaded when the app runs.

Apart from the detailed drawings above, the full commercial controller drawings of the main pack are **not** redistributed: its README says most are taken or recreated from official sources, and the global MIT licence does not establish the rights on each manufacturer image. Users can import a drawing of their choice instead.

## Fonts

- **Inter Regular / SemiBold**: `fonts/Inter-Regular.ttf` and `fonts/Inter-SemiBold.ttf`, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`fonts/OFL.txt`). Their internal family name, `Inter 18pt`, is the one used in the stylesheet.
- **Matcha Style**: original file by Khurasan Studio, shipped as `fonts/MatchaStyle.ttf` and used for the app title. The author states "100% free for personal use & commercial use"; the archive has no separate licence. Its origin and that statement are kept in `fonts/MatchaStyle-NOTICE.txt`, without relicensing it under GPL or OFL.

Qt loads the three fonts from the bundled files with `QFontDatabase.addApplicationFont` before creating any window; installed fonts are not needed.
